# Geometric Quantization

I gave a talk to Math graduate students in Boğaziçi about Geometric Quantization, focusing to WKB approximation and its connection with Lagrangian submanifolds in symplectic geometry. This is my notes on this topic, mainly based on the book 'Lectures on the Geometry of Quantization' of Weinstein and Bates.
